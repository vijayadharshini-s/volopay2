Avoiding false alarms

Before outreach, check seasonal patterns, holidays, recent entity migrations, changed spending policy, and whether spend moved to another Volopay entity. Mark an account “Needs review” so the score is not treated as a confirmed churn signal.

Prototype limitations

Scores are calculated from illustrative mock signals, not real Volopay data. In production, use a rolling baseline and compare like-for-like periods.
